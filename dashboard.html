Directions

BSL → English

1Select the BSL → English panel.
2Upload a BSL video or type glosses in the input.
3Click Recognise BSL signs or Translate.
4You will see the English meaning and can listen to speech output.

English → BSL

1Select the English → BSL panel.
2Record your voice or type in English.
3Click Convert to BSL.
4You will see BSL glosses and a 2D skeleton or 3D avatar animation.

About BSL word order

BSL uses different word order from English. "What time is the meeting tomorrow?" becomes TOMORROW MEETING WHAT TIME in BSL. This is correct — BSL has its own grammar.

Tips for best results

Sign clearly against a plain, well-lit background. Keep both hands fully visible. For speech, speak clearly in a quiet place. Short phrases give better results than long sentences.
